Dryland Performance

Population 9,000 – 15,600  •  Non-irrigated commercial fields

Key Trends

  • AcreMax dominates dryland: P1122AML leads at 67.5 bu/a on 623 acres. P0075AM follows at 60.8 bu/a on 1,146 acres — the largest dryland acreage in the territory.
  • Population sweet spot: All top performers planted 12,200–13,800 seeds/acre. No yield advantage observed above 14,000 in dryland conditions.
  • Platform story: AcreMax/AM hybrids fill 5 of the top 6 spots. PowerCore Enlist and Vorceed entries have limited dryland acreage — they're built for irrigated environments.
  • P0157AMXT: Most-planted dryland hybrid (1,623 ac) averaged 34.7 bu/a — widespread adoption but a tough year for this maturity.

Irrigated Performance

Population 18,000 – 40,000  •  Full and limited irrigation combined

Key Trends

  • P10300PCE leads on high-confidence acres: 275.5 bu/a on 1,771 acres — the highest yield with 1,000+ acres behind it. PowerCore Enlist platform proving out.
  • P14364PCUE & P14830AML strong: 264.9 and 261.8 bu/a respectively, both on 1,700+ acres. Consistent high-volume performance.

  • Population range 31–33K: Top performers cluster in this range. No consistent yield advantage pushing above 33,000.
  • P12517PCUE & P14270PCE top the chart at 292.3 and 288.2 bu/a, but on limited acres (33 and 17 ac). Worth watching as acreage grows.

About This Data

Aggregated from commercial production fields across McConnell Enterprises, McDaniel Enterprises, and Vieselmeyer Seed territory (Logan, Phillips, Sedgwick counties). 2026 crop year, Pioneer brand only.

Yield and seeding rate are as-reported from combine and planter data. Dryland entries require a minimum of 10 harvest acres; irrigated entries require 15 acres. Entries below these thresholds are shown with reduced emphasis.

This data reflects actual grower results from a wide range of field conditions, soil types, and management practices. It is not a controlled experiment. Use alongside test plot data and multi-year performance records for product positioning decisions.
