WeatherLink Setup

Create a free account, sign in, and find weather stations near you. Takes about 3 minutes.

1

Download the App

Get the free WeatherLink app on your phone. You can also use the website, but the app is faster for checking conditions in the field.

Download for iPhone Download for Android

Free — no subscription required

2

Create Your Account

  1. Open the app (or go to weatherlink.com)
  2. Tap Sign Up Free
  3. Enter your name, email, and a password
  4. Check your email and verify your account
You don't need to own a station to create an account. Anyone can sign up for free and browse nearby stations.
3

Sign In

Open the app and sign in with the email and password you just created. On the website, go to:

weatherlink.com
4

Find Stations Near You

Once signed in, use the map view to browse weather stations in your area. Every green dot is a station sharing live data.

  1. Tap the map icon (or "Explore" on the website)
  2. Zoom into your area — each dot is a live weather station
  3. Tap a station to see its current conditions — temp, humidity, wind, rain, barometric pressure
  4. Tap the star to add it to your favorites for quick access later
Look for stations near your fields. Conditions can vary a lot across even 10 miles in NE Colorado. Favorite the stations closest to where you farm.
5

What You Can See

Every station shows live data. Stations with solar/UV sensors (like a Vantage Pro2 Plus) also show ET — critical for irrigation decisions.

Temperature

Current, high, low — updated every 2.5 seconds on local WiFi

Wind

Speed, gusts, direction — real-time

Rain

Daily total, rain rate, storm total

ET

Evapotranspiration — daily crop water use (Pro2 Plus stations only)
