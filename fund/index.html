How it works

Plant the seed

Credit is earned per bag on every unit booked through us. Bags are tallied at settlement, so what you plant this spring shows up on the fall statement.

It stacks up

Balances roll forward year over year. Nothing expires and nothing is lost if you don't spend it — several accounts have been building since before 2021.

Spend it your way

Take it at the ToGo Ag counter, put it against coats and gear, direct it to a college fund, or send it to the hospital foundation or a local cause. Your call.
