Before You Start

What You Need

  • Smartphone with Bluetooth (iPhone or Android)
  • EnviroMonitor app installed (download before heading to the field)
  • Free WeatherLink account (create in the app or at weatherlink.com)
  • Credit card for activation fee and annual service plan
  • Mounting poles or existing structures at each field location
  • Phillips-head screwdriver
  • Adequate cellular coverage at each Gateway location
Download the apps first. Get the EnviroMonitor app on your phone while you still have WiFi. You will need it to activate each Gateway via Bluetooth in the field.

Activate the Gateway

Power Up & Connect

Each Gateway is solar-powered with a built-in 5-watt panel and rechargeable 6-volt backup battery. No AC power needed.

  1. Unbox the Gateway and verify all mounting hardware is included
  2. Plug the main power cable into the jack to power up the Gateway
  3. The blue LED will begin flashing — this means Bluetooth is ready to pair
  4. Open the EnviroMonitor app on your phone (Bluetooth must be on)
  5. Log in with your WeatherLink account (or tap Sign Up to create one)
  6. Tap Add Gateway and bring your phone close to the unit
  7. The app will connect via Bluetooth and walk you through activation
  8. Select your update interval (how often data uploads via cellular)
  9. Pay the one-time activation fee (~$35) and select your annual service plan

LED Indicators

Blue flashing: Bluetooth ready to pair with your phone

Blue solid: Successfully connected to your phone

Cell LED green: Good cellular coverage — data will upload

Cell LED red: Poor cellular coverage — try relocating

Repeat for all 7 Gateways. Each one gets its own activation. All Gateways live under the same WeatherLink account, so you manage everything from one login.

Annual Service Plans

Mount the Gateway

Placement Guidelines

  • Height: Mount above the crop canopy for best cellular and mesh network signal
  • Solar panel: Orient to face south for maximum sun exposure
  • Spacing: Keep the Gateway and any cabled sensor suite at least 4 feet apart on the pole so the ISS doesn't shadow the Gateway's solar panel at midday
  • Cellular: Verify the cell LED shows green after power-up. If it blinks red, the location has poor coverage — try higher or move to a different spot
Long-range antenna upgrade available. The Gateway comes with a standard dipole antenna. Upgrading to a long-range antenna extends mesh network range from 1,000 ft up to 10,000 ft (3,000 m). Remove the smoky plastic cover with a Phillips screwdriver, unscrew the stock antenna, and attach the long-range replacement.
4

Connect Cabled Sensor Suites (6327C)

Cabled VP2 Plus → Gateway Direct Connection

Your 7 cabled VP2 Plus sensor suites connect directly to the Gateway — no Node required. Each Gateway gets one cabled ISS.

  1. Remove the plug from one of the cable entry holes in the bottom of the Gateway housing
  2. Run the 100-foot RJ11 cable from the sensor suite up through the hole
  3. Plug the RJ11 connector into the ISS cable jack on the Gateway board
  4. Mount the sensor suite on a pole at least 4 feet away from the Gateway
  5. The Gateway will immediately begin receiving data — updates every 2.5 seconds

What the 6327C Measures

Sensor What It Reads
Temperature & HumidityOutside temp, humidity in passive radiation shield
AnemometerWind speed, gusts, and direction
Rain GaugeRainfall rate, daily total, storm total (AeroCone collector)
UV SensorUV index — crop stress monitoring
Solar RadiationSolar energy — critical for ET calculations

Sensor Suite Mounting

  • Temperature/humidity: 5–6 feet above ground in the radiation shield
  • Anemometer: Ideally 30 ft above ground for standard readings; crop-canopy height is fine for microclimate monitoring
  • Rain gauge: Unobstructed from trees and buildings — the AeroCone collector sits at ~4.6 ft when the temp sensor is at standard height
  • UV & solar sensors: Must have an unobstructed sky view — no shadows from structures or trees
Cabled ISS = no wireless interference. The 100-foot cable provides a rock-solid, interference-free connection. Ideal when the sensor suite is close to the Gateway.
5

Connect Wireless Sensor Suites (6322)

Wireless VP2 Range & Placement

Your 3 wireless VP2 sensor suites transmit at 902–928 MHz using frequency-hopping spread spectrum (FHSS). They need to be within range of a Gateway or Node.

Condition Expected Range
Line of sight (open field)1,000 ft (300 m)
Above crop canopy (corn/sorghum)1,700–2,000 ft
Through buildings/obstructions200–400 ft

Transmitter ID Setup

Each wireless ISS uses a transmitter ID (1–8) to identify itself. If multiple wireless units are in range of the same Gateway, each must have a unique ID.

  1. The default transmitter ID is 1
  2. Set unique IDs using the DIP switches on the ISS circuit board
  3. For your 3 units, set them to IDs 1, 2, and 3
  4. Register each ID in the EnviroMonitor app when adding the sensor
Need more range? Add EnviroMonitor Nodes (SKU 6810) between the wireless ISS and the Gateway to relay data through the mesh network. Nodes can extend your reach across large fields.
6

Verify Data is Flowing

Check Each Station

  1. Open the EnviroMonitor app and confirm all Gateways show as online
  2. For each Gateway, verify sensor data is populating (temperature, wind, rain)
  3. Open WeatherLink.com in a browser and confirm your stations appear with live data
  4. Open the WeatherLink app on your phone — all stations should show current conditions
  5. Open the Mobilize app and set up your field views
Data takes a few minutes to appear on WeatherLink.com after activation. If a station doesn't show up after 15 minutes, check the cellular LED on the Gateway and verify the cable/wireless connections.

Set Up Mobilize for Agriculture

Mobilize: Your Field Decision App

Mobilize turns raw weather data into actionable farm intelligence. Set it up once and it runs automatically.

Create Field Views

  1. Open Mobilize and log in with your WeatherLink account
  2. Tap Add Field and draw your field boundary on the map
  3. Assign the nearest weather station to each field
  4. Select your crop type for each field

What Mobilize Tracks

Irrigation

ET data, past and forecast rain, irrigation scheduling alerts. Know when and where to water.

Frost Alerts

Set temperature thresholds. Get an alert when it's time to head to the field and intervene.

Growing Degree Days

Track GDD against custom targets by crop type. 7-day GDD forecast included.

Pest Management

Timely risk reports on pest damage so you can act before crops are harmed. (Pro+ subscription)
