How Pioneer Names Its Corn

Every Pioneer hybrid number tells you four things at a glance: the brand, maturity, unique genetics, and trait package.

Example: Current Product
P12345V
Pioneer Brand CRM (Maturity) Random Number Trait ID

P — Pioneer Brand

Identifies the product as a Pioneer brand corn hybrid.

CRM — Maturity

Based on family genetics. Tells you the approximate Comparative Relative Maturity of the hybrid’s “platform.”

Random Number

01–99 for current products, 01–999 for newly advanced. Multiple genetics can share the same CRM.

Trait Identifier

The smaller letters at the end tell you which insect protection and herbicide tolerance traits are in the bag.

Example: Newly Advanced Product (5-digit)
P12345PCUE
Same CRM, more room for unique numbers (up to 999)

Refuge & What’s In The Bag

Pioneer products come in two refuge configurations. Integrated refuge is blended in the bag. Structured refuge is 100% treated.

Integrated Refuge (In-Bag)

AM, AML, AMX, AMXT, Q, V

Major component treated for insect protection. Minor component (5–10%) is refuge seed blended in the bag.

Additional 20% corn borer refuge is required in EPA-designated cotton counties.

Structured Refuge (100% Treated)

PCE, PCUE, PWE, PWUE, YHR, VYHR

Every seed in the bag is treated. Grower plants a separate 5% structured refuge in the corn belt (20% in EPA cotton counties).

Structured products are treated 100% — no blended refuge component.
